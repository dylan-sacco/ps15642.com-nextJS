## Why Professional Landscaping Pays for Itself

Studies consistently show that professional landscaping returns 100–200% of its cost in home value at the time of sale. But the benefits go beyond resale:

- **Lower utility bills** — strategic tree and shrub placement reduces summer cooling costs by shading your home
- **Reduced erosion** — proper grading and plantings prevent soil loss and protect your foundation
- **Less maintenance over time** — a well-designed landscape with the right plants for your zone means less watering, pruning, and replanting
- **Improved drainage** — poor drainage is one of the most common and damaging property problems in Western PA; landscaping can fix it

## Landscaping Ideas That Work in Western Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania's climate — cold winters, hot summers, and plenty of rain — means not every plant or design works here. These are approaches that thrive in Westmoreland County conditions.

### Native Plant Landscaping

Native plants are adapted to Pennsylvania's climate and require far less maintenance than imported ornamentals. Consider:

- **Black-eyed Susans** and **Purple Coneflower** for low-maintenance color
- **Pennsylvania Sedge** as a ground cover alternative to turf grass in shaded areas
- **Serviceberry and Redbud trees** for year-round visual interest
- **Wild Bergamot** to attract pollinators and add fragrance

Native plantings also support local wildlife and are naturally drought-resistant once established.

![Native plants and perennial garden border along a home exterior](https://picsum.photos/id/237/800/500)

### Defined Garden Beds With Clean Edging

One of the highest-impact, most affordable landscaping upgrades is simply defining your garden beds with clean, crisp edging. It instantly makes your property look more polished and intentional.

Pair sharp bed edges with a layer of fresh mulch (2–3 inches deep) to suppress weeds, retain moisture, and give beds a finished appearance that lasts all season.

### Foundation Planting for Curb Appeal

Foundation plantings — the shrubs and plants directly around your home's foundation — are critical to curb appeal. Common mistakes include:

- Planting too close to the house (blocks ventilation, promotes moisture damage)
- Choosing plants that grow too large and eventually crowd windows
- Mixing too many species with no cohesion

A professional landscape design creates intentional layers: low ground cover at the front, medium shrubs in the middle, and taller accent plants at corners and entryways.

### Outdoor Living Spaces

The most popular landscaping trend in Western PA is extending usable outdoor living space. This means integrating:

- **Patio areas** with seating, fire pits, or outdoor kitchens
- **Pathway systems** connecting your driveway, entry, and backyard
- **Privacy screens** using fast-growing evergreens like Emerald Green Arborvitae or Leyland Cypress
- **Focal point trees** that anchor the design and provide seasonal color

These elements turn a yard into an outdoor room you can enjoy from spring through fall.

## Landscaping Maintenance: Keeping It Looking Great

![Landscaper mulching and trimming a professionally designed garden bed](https://picsum.photos/id/390/800/500)

Even the best landscape design requires regular upkeep. A basic annual maintenance schedule for Westmoreland County includes:

| Season | Key Tasks |
|--------|-----------|
| Spring | Cleanup, fresh mulch, fertilization, pruning winter damage |
| Summer | Mowing, edging, irrigation, deadheading flowers |
| Fall | Leaf removal, aerating, overseeding, cutting back perennials |
| Winter | Snow removal, protecting sensitive plantings |
