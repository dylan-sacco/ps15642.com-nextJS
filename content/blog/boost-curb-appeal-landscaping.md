Real estate professionals consistently say it: curb appeal sells homes. But even if you're not planning to sell, a well-maintained and thoughtfully designed exterior makes your home more enjoyable to live in, reduces maintenance costs over time, and builds equity.

In Westmoreland County, where neighborhoods range from older established communities to newer developments, the properties that stand out are the ones where every outdoor element — lawn, landscape, hardscape, and structure — works together as a cohesive whole.

Here are ten curb appeal upgrades that deliver real value, ranked loosely from quick wins to bigger investments.

![Beautiful home exterior with manicured lawn, lush landscaping, and clean hardscape](https://picsum.photos/id/1/800/500)

## 1. Professional Lawn Care

Nothing else you do to your home's exterior has as much visual impact as a thick, green, well-edged lawn. A struggling lawn — full of weeds, bare patches, and uneven color — undermines everything else you invest in.

Professional lawn care that includes regular fertilization, weed control, and sharp mowing edges costs far less than most homeowners expect. The return in visual impact is immediate. If your lawn is beyond routine maintenance, a fall renovation program (core aeration + overseeding) can transform even the most damaged lawn in a single season.

**ROI estimate:** Studies show a well-maintained lawn returns up to 200% of landscaping investment at resale.

## 2. Fresh Mulch in All Beds

This is the fastest and most affordable upgrade on this list. A 2–3 inch layer of fresh dark mulch in your garden beds:

- Makes plantings look immediately more intentional and polished
- Suppresses weeds for the season
- Retains soil moisture (reducing your watering needs)
- Provides slow nutrients as it decomposes

A single day's work — or a half-day with a professional crew — can transform the appearance of every bed on your property. Do this every spring.

## 3. Defined Bed Edging

Paired with fresh mulch, clean bed edging is transformative. The difference between a bed with crisp edges and one where grass and mulch blur together is dramatic. A steel half-moon edger or bed edging machine cuts a clean line that holds all season.

For a permanent solution, consider installing steel or aluminum landscape edging that eliminates the need to re-edge each year.

![Freshly mulched and edged garden bed along a home foundation with colorful perennials](https://picsum.photos/id/177/800/500)

## 4. Foundation Planting Refresh

Many older homes in Westmoreland County have original foundation plantings that have outgrown their space — overgrown junipers touching the windows, arborvitae blocking the entry, shrubs against the siding. These plantings that once looked great now make the home look dated and poorly maintained.

A foundation planting refresh involves:
- Removing overgrown, dated, or inappropriate plants
- Designing a layered replacement planting with scale-appropriate species
- Introducing seasonal color with perennials or annuals near the entry

This single change can make a home look 20 years newer.

## 5. A Defined Front Walkway

If your front walkway is cracked concrete, bare dirt, or stepping stones that have seen better days, replacing it with a clean brick or paver walkway is one of the best curb appeal investments you can make. A well-designed walkway:

- Creates a clear, welcoming path to the entry
- Frames the front lawn
- Adds immediate "designed" feel to the entire property

Pair a new walkway with low-growing ground cover plantings or a border of perennials along the edges to soften the hardscape and add seasonal color.

## 6. Landscape Lighting

Low-voltage landscape lighting is frequently overlooked — and massively undervalued. Good outdoor lighting:

- Showcases your property after dark (important for evening house hunters)
- Creates safety along walkways and steps
- Highlights specimen trees, architectural features, and planting beds
- Extends the outdoor living season into evening hours

Modern LED landscape lighting systems are inexpensive to run, easy to install, and available with smart controls. Path lights, uplighting on trees and architectural features, and step lighting are the three highest-impact applications.

![Outdoor landscape lighting illuminating a pathway and trees at dusk](https://picsum.photos/id/239/800/500)

## 7. Clean Gutters and Downspouts

This one isn't glamorous, but clogged gutters overflow, stain your siding, and direct water toward your foundation — leading to erosion, basement moisture problems, and plant damage. Clean gutters twice a year: once in late spring after trees have fully leafed out, and once in late fall after leaves have dropped.

While you're at it, check downspout extensions. Water should discharge at least 4–6 feet from the foundation to protect your basement and yard grading.

## 8. Tree and Shrub Pruning

Overgrown, unpruned trees and shrubs signal neglect immediately to any passerby. An annual or biannual pruning program keeps plantings in scale with your property, removes deadwood, and encourages healthy, attractive growth habits.

Specific high-impact pruning targets for curb appeal:
- Raise the crown of any trees overhanging the roofline or blocking sightlines to the home
- Remove crossing branches and water sprouts from ornamental trees
- Rejuvenate woody shrubs that have lost their shape with selective thinning cuts

## 9. A Patio or Outdoor Living Area

For overall property value (not just curb appeal from the street), a well-designed backyard patio is one of the strongest-returning improvements you can make. Homes with functional outdoor living spaces consistently command higher sale prices in the current market.

A paver patio, fire pit area, or simple seating space signals livability and adds square footage — in buyers' minds — beyond what's inside the house. If your backyard is currently an underused lawn, this investment is worth serious consideration.

## 10. Exterior Paint or Stain on Wood Features

Peeling or faded paint on a deck, fence, shutters, or trim is one of the fastest ways to make a home look neglected. A fresh coat of exterior paint or deck stain:

- Immediately improves the visual appearance
- Protects wood from moisture damage
- Signals that the property has been consistently maintained

For decks specifically, plan to clean and reseal or stain every 2–3 years in our climate. Pennsylvania's temperature swings and precipitation are hard on outdoor wood.
