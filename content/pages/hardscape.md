## Concrete Patios

A poured concrete patio is one of the better investments you can make in a backyard. It's durable, low maintenance, and gives you a solid base for furniture, a grill, whatever you're putting out there. We can do a standard broom finish or stamped patterns if you want something with a bit more character.

Sizing and layout get figured out on-site. We'll mark it out, walk through it with you, and make sure it connects to the house or yard the way you're picturing before any concrete gets poured.

## Sidewalks and Walkways

Cracked or heaving concrete is a trip hazard — and it looks rough. We do both new installations and replacements. If only a few sections of an existing walk are bad, we can pull and replace just what needs to go rather than tearing up the whole thing.

We use expansion joints in the right places so the slab has somewhere to move without cracking, and we get our mix delivered fresh same-day.

## Other Work

We've also done fire pit surrounds, retaining walls, steps, and various other concrete and masonry work around the yard. If you have something in mind that doesn't fit neatly into one of those categories, just reach out and describe it — chances are we've done something similar.
