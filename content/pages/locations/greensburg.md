## Serving Greensburg & Westmoreland County

As the county seat of Westmoreland County, Greensburg is home to a mix of historic residential neighborhoods, commercial properties, and municipal spaces — all of which require quality outdoor maintenance and improvement. P&S Contracting and Landscape serves the Greensburg area with the same professionalism and craftsmanship we have delivered across western Pennsylvania since 2007.

## Our Services

We handle a wide range of outdoor work for both residential and commercial clients in the Greensburg area:

- **Lawn Care & Maintenance** — mowing, edging, fertilization, and seasonal treatments
- **Landscaping** — design, installation, mulching, planting beds, and garden renovation
- **Hardscaping** — custom patios, walkways, retaining walls, and decorative stonework
- **Tree & Stump Removal** — professional removal of trees of any size, stump grinding
- **Decks & Railings** — deck construction and replacement, aluminum and composite railing
- **Commercial Grounds Maintenance** — recurring contracts for business properties, parking lots, and common areas

## Residential & Commercial

We work with both homeowners looking to improve their outdoor living space and commercial property managers who need reliable, consistent grounds care. Whether it is a backyard patio project or a multi-property lawn maintenance contract, we have the crew and equipment to handle it.
