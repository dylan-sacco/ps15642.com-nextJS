Tree work is something we've been doing for a while now. We handle removals, trimming, and stump grinding for homeowners all around North Huntingdon and Westmoreland County.

---

## Tree Removal

If you have a tree that's dead, leaning the wrong way, or just too close to the house, we can take it down safely. We're used to working in tight spots — close to structures, over fences, near power lines. Cleanup is part of the job. We haul everything out and leave the yard clean when we're done.

## Trimming and Pruning

Sometimes a tree just needs some work — dead branches cleared out, weight redistributed so it doesn't split in a storm, or limbs pulled away from the roof or siding. A well-trimmed tree is a lot healthier and causes far fewer headaches when the weather gets bad.

## Stump Grinding

After a tree comes down, you're left with a stump. A lot of people live with them longer than they should. We grind them down below grade so you can seed over the area or do whatever you want with the space. It's faster than most people expect, and the difference it makes in the yard is immediate.

Stumps from old removals are fine too — doesn't have to be something we cut down.

## Storm Cleanup

If a tree comes down on your property after a storm, give us a call. We try to get out to those situations quickly.
