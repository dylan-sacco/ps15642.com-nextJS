We do landscape design, planting, and cleanup work for homes in North Huntingdon and the surrounding area. A lot of our jobs are either starting fresh on a new yard or clearing out what's there and replacing it with something that actually fits the space.

---

## Removal and Cleanup

Before anything new goes in, the old stuff has to come out. We handle shrub and small tree removal, old bed cleanup, and whatever else is in the way. We haul it all off and leave the area ready to work with.

## Design and Planting

We design around what you actually want the yard to look like and what's practical to maintain. Western PA has real seasons, so we think about what works year-round rather than just what looks good in June. We use a mix of perennials and shrubs that come back on their own, with annuals for color where it makes sense.

What we typically work with:

- Shrubs and ornamental trees
- Perennial beds (come back year after year)
- Seasonal color plantings
- Mulch and edging

## Outdoor Lighting

We install landscape lighting for pathways, beds, and outdoor living areas. Most installs are low-voltage LED — reliable, use almost no power, and look good. Good lighting makes a real difference in how a yard looks in the evening and adds some practical safety to walkways and steps.
